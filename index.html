STELLAR TRADER

// vector edition — 200X //

Trade your way across a small star cluster. Buy low, sell high,
bank credits, and trade up to bigger, meaner ships at the shipyard.
Pirates roam the lanes — they'll chase you and blow you apart, so shoot back.

Each run you begin docked at a random home base — buy cargo and pick your
ship in safety. Nothing can touch you until you undock into open space.
